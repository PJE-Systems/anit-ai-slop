# Motion Review

Motion should communicate:

- hierarchy
- process
- movement
- spatial relationships
- interaction feedback
- meaningful transitions

Warning signs:

- every element fades in
- every card has a hover lift
- background constantly moves
- random parallax
- cursor-following decoration
- excessive blur

If motion does not explain, guide or enhance an interaction, question whether it is necessary.

For genuine cinematic storytelling, combine with `cinematic-web-motion`.
