# Visual Design

Create a visual language that belongs to the brand rather than to a trend.

Evaluate:

- color
- typography
- spacing
- shape language
- imagery
- texture
- depth
- contrast
- composition

Before choosing a style, identify what the company physically does, which environments and objects are associated with it, what emotions are appropriate, and what existing brand assets must remain.

A strong design contains decisions that would make little sense for an unrelated company.

Premium design often comes from removing things. If gradients, glow, blur, shadows, 3D, particles and animated text are all present, question whether all are necessary.
