# Anti-Slop Copywriting

Write for the actual customer, not the template.

Avoid vague language such as:

- innovative solutions
- seamless service
- cutting-edge technology
- powerful platform
- next-generation
- unlock your potential

Prefer concrete descriptions of:

- what is provided
- who receives it
- how it works
- what problem it solves
- what the customer should do next

Every factual claim must come from verified project/source material. Never invent numbers to fill a layout.
