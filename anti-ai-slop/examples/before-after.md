# Before / After

## Generic AI version

Hero: "Empowering the future with innovative solutions."

Visual: gradient background, glowing orb and floating glass card.

Below: three identical cards, then three more cards, then testimonial cards.

Problem: nothing is specific to the company.

## Anti-slop version

Start from the actual business model.

Use a concrete proposition, relevant imagery, a real customer problem, the actual service process, real proof when available and a clear action.

The design may use fewer effects but feel more distinctive because the concept is specific.

Do not make a generic design less generic by adding more effects. Change the underlying concept.
